## Introduction

This modpack is customized for [Lucky World Invasion](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/lucky-world-invasion), that locks in Hardcore Mode and is more vanilla. There are no Mushroom Kingdom, waystone and guns, ect, and now you need to complete the challenge in a more hard condition.

Recommended by [Laink](http://www.youtube.com/@WankilFr)

## Customized Content

-   Remove mods like tacz, Touhou Little Maid that disrupt the vanilla experience.
-   Keep QoL mods like backpacks.
-   Appropriate difficulty increase.
-   The lucky blocks that was originally in the Mushroom Kingdom will now be in the overworld.
